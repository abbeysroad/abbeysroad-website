ABOUT PANDORA TRAVEL

Nordic Travel Market Intelligence & Commercial Network

Strategic Coordination from Copenhagen

Operating from our corporate office in Copenhagen, Denmark, Pandora Travel acts as a local representative for international tourism brands. We maintain active pipelines and close personal connections with leading Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, and Finnish tour operators, ensuring efficient contract matching and streamlined communication.

Our operations ensure that all our destination supplier standards align perfectly with European consumer expectations, guest safety requirements, and sustainable tourism criteria.

Our Ground-Handling Alliances

We partner with carefully selected DMCs and inbound tour operators at each destination. We choose and represent varies partners on each destinations, providing support in showcasing their products and services. Our partners are therefore chosen based on their local expertise, destination knowledge, and ability to provide the right level of service and experiences for each type of stay.

We closely collaborate with DOT - OMAN HERITAGE AND TOURISM AUTHORITY, NORDIC CHAMBERS OF COMMERCE, ASEAN DMCs & TOs ASSOCIATION to promote the destinations, regulations, travel updates and trends.

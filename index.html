DAY 2 | Royalty, Canals & Historic Cobblestone

🇸🇪 Stockholm to Karlstad: The Swedish Transition

The Vibe

Explore a city built across 14 islands before winding down by the scenic shores of Lake Vänern.

📋 Today's Plan
  • 🏰
    Stockholm City Tour

    Wander the medieval alleyways of Gamla Stan (Old Town), view the Royal Palace, the grand Opera House, and catch panoramic views from Fjällgatan Hill.

  • 🚌
    Scenic Countryside Drive

    Relax on a panoramic 2.5-hour coach ride (225 km) deep into the Swedish countryside to Karlstad.

  • 🍽️
    Welcome Dinner

    Regroup for an authentic dinner at a local Karlstad restaurant.

🏨 Stay: Good Morning Karlstad (or similar)
3

DAY 3 | Sculpture Parks & Majestic Fjords

🇳🇴 Crossing Borders: Karlstad to Oslo & Ål

The Vibe

Transition from Sweden to Norway. Experience masterfully crafted art parks before heading into the gateway of the majestic Norwegian fjords.

📋 Today's Plan
  • 🌳
    Oslo City Tour

    Walk among the 200+ mesmerizing masterworks in Vigeland Sculpture Park and Frogner Park, stand outside the Oslo Cathedral, and feel the energy of Karl Johans gate.

  • 📸
    Holmenkollen Panoramic View

    Capture a panoramic snapshot at the world-famous Holmenkollen ski jump area overlooking the city and fjords.

  • 🏔️
    Into the Highlands

    Board your long-distance coach for a scenic 3-hour journey (195 km) to the mountain village of Ål.

  • 🪵
    Alpine Feast

    Indulge in a premium 3-course or buffet dinner hosted at your hotel.

🏨 Stay: Thon Hotel Hallingdal (or similar)
4

DAY 4 | The Crown Jewel of Rail Journeys

🚂 The Flåm Railway Experience

The Vibe

Prepare for one of the absolute highlights of your trip—a jaw-dropping train ride through steep ravines, cascading waterfalls, and deep fjords.

📋 Today's Plan
  • 🚂
    The Legendary Flåm Railway

    Board the legendary Flåm-Myrdal loop. Peer out panoramic windows as you ascend over 800 meters along the sheer walls of the valley.

  • 🍽️
    Fjord-Side Lunch

    Savor local specialties at a charming local restaurant in the heart of the valley.

  • 🏔️
    Highland Return

    Return to the serene comfort of Ål for another incredible 3-course hotel dinner.

🏨 Stay: Thon Hotel Hallingdal (or similar)

DAY 7 | Of Castles, Queens & Open Sandwiches

The Vibe

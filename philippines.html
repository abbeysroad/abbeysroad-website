Palawan · Western Philippines

Glide through towering karst limestone cathedrals, kayak inside emerald secret lagoons, and discover untouched white sand beaches tucked behind dramatic cliff formations.

Best known for: Big Lagoon, Miniloc karst cliffs, Island hopping
Ideal for: Couples · Adventurers · Kayakers

CRYSTAL LAKES & SHIPWRECKS

CORON

Calamian Islands · Palawan

Dive beneath mirror-like waters to explore preserved WWII Japanese shipwrecks, swim through thermocline lakes like Kayangan and Barracuda, and sail past dramatic cliff sanctuaries.

Best known for: Kayangan Lake, WWII Wreck Diving, Coral Gardens
Ideal for: Divers · Snorkelers · Underwater Photographers
Suggested stay: 3–4 Days
Best time: October – June

BORACAY

Aklan · Western Visayas

Feel powdery soft white sand beneath your feet, sail on iconic blue-and-white Paraw boats into flaming golden sunsets, and indulge in beachfront dining under coconut palms.

Best known for: 4-Mile White Beach, Paraw Sunset Sailing, Nightlife
Ideal for: Beach lovers · Sunseekers · Couples & Luxury
Suggested stay: 3–5 Days
Best time: November – May

Ride world-famous Cloud 9 waves, cruise down endless roads lined with millions of coconut palms, discover natural rock pools, and immerse yourself in laid-back island café culture.

Best known for: Cloud 9 Surf Break, Coconut Viewpoint, Sugba Lagoon
Ideal for: Surfers · Digital Nomads · Free Spirits
Suggested stay: 4–7 Days
Best time: March – November

Central Visayas

Dive into the breathtaking Moalboal sardine run, swim under turquoise waterfalls at Kawasan, explore historic 16th-century Magellan's Cross, and jump across island reefs.

Best known for: Sardine Run, Kawasan Canyoneering, Magellan's Cross
Ideal for: Central Visayas · Freedivers · Eco-adventurers
Suggested stay: 3–5 Days
Best time: Year-round

BOHOL

Central Visayas

Marvel at over 1,200 symmetrical Chocolate Hills, meet the world's smallest primate — the Philippine Tarsier, cruise along the emerald Loboc River, and relax on Panglao Island.

Best known for: Chocolate Hills, Tarsier Sanctuary, Loboc River Cruise
Ideal for: Families · Nature Enthusiasts · Countryside Explorers
Suggested stay: 2–3 Days
Best time: November – May
EXPLORE BOHOL →

BANAUE & CORDILLERAS

Ifugao · Northern Luzon

Trek through 2,000-year-old rice terrace amphitheaters carved by hand into misty green mountain ridges, stay in indigenous villages, and breathe crisp mountain air.

Best known for: Batad Rice Terraces, Ifugao Heritage, Mountain Trekking
Ideal for: Hikers · Photographers · Cultural Travelers
Suggested stay: 3–4 Days
Best time: December – May

Best known for: Perfect Cone Volcano, Lava ATV Trails, Spicy Food

Suggested stay: 2–3 Days
Best time: January – May

VENICE OF THE NORTH

STOCKHOLM

Capital Archipelago · Sweden

Explore a stunning metropolis built across 14 islands, featuring the grand medieval alleyways of Gamla Stan, the historic royal palaces, and the majestic Vasa museum.

Best known for: Gamla Stan, Vasa Museum, Stockholm Palace
Ideal for: Cultural enthusiasts · Scenic walkers · Historians
Suggested stay: 2–3 Days
Best time: June – August

GATEWAY TO THE FJORDLANDS

OSLO

Capital Municipality · Oslofjord · Norway

Transition to the art and design hub of Norway. Walk among the 200+ giant granite statues of Vigeland Sculpture Park, visit Oslo Cathedral, and stand above the Holmenkollen ski jump.

Best known for: Vigeland Art Park, Holmenkollen Summit, Karl Johans gate
Ideal for: Architecture lovers · Photographers · Art fans
Suggested stay: 1–2 Days
Best time: May – September
EXPLORE OSLO →
THE SCENIC CROWN JEWEL

FLÅM & FJORDS

Aurlandsfjord · Valley & Railways · Norway

Ascend sheer canyon walls along the legendary Flåm Mountain Railway. Peer down breathtaking green ravines and cruise deep water bays surrounded by cascading waterfalls.

Best known for: Flåm Mountain Railway, Nærøyfjord, Aurlandsfjord
Ideal for: Nature seekers · Train lovers · Adventure travelers
Suggested stay: 2 Days
Best time: June – September
EXPLORE FLÅM →
MEDIEVAL TRADING WHARF

BERGEN

Hanseatic Port · West Coast · Norway

Wander among the iconic colorful wooden townhouses of Bryggen, ride the steep Fløibanen funicular for sky-high views, and sample fresh seafood at the local fish market.

Best known for: Bryggen Wharf, Fløibanen Funicular, Fish Market
Ideal for: Foodies · Hikers · Maritime historians
Suggested stay: 2 Days
Best time: May – August
EXPLORE BERGEN →
CAPITAL OF HYGGE

COPENHAGEN

Capital Municipality · Oresund Strait · Denmark

Wake up in Denmark. Cycle down pedestrian-friendly canals, relax in the glowing lights of Tivoli Gardens, visit Kronborg Castle, and feast on traditional open sandwiches.

Best known for: Nyhavn Canal, Tivoli Gardens, Kronborg Castle, Smørrebrød
Ideal for: Bicycle lovers · Foodies · Royal historians
Suggested stay: 2–3 Days
Best time: May – September
EXPLORE COPENHAGEN →
THE BALTIC SEASIDE CITY

HELSINKI

Capital Area · Gulf of Finland

Discover the unique design lines of Helsinki. Tour the breathtaking Rock Church carved directly into solid stone, see the red brick walls of Uspenski Cathedral, and relax in seaside saunas.

Best known for: Rock Church, Uspenski Cathedral, Harbor Market
Ideal for: Architecture fans · Spa lovers · Seafood foodies
Suggested stay: 1–2 Days
Best time: June – September

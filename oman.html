PORT OF HERITAGE

MUSCAT

Capital Municipality · Gulf of Oman

Witness a white-walled city nestled between volcanic mountains and the blue sea — featuring the grand marble halls of Sultan Qaboos Mosque and the colorful Muttrah Souk.

Best known for: Grand Mosque, Muttrah Souk, Opera House
Ideal for: Cultural enthusiasts · Foodies · Walkers
Suggested stay: 2–3 Days
Best time: October – April

THE GREEN MOUNTAIN

JEBEL AKHDAR

Al Hajar Mountains · 2000m Altitude

Escape to cool breezes and canyon edge views. Stay at world-class resorts, hike between stone villages, and breathe the scent of damask rose water farms.

Best known for: Canyons, Rosewater farming, Mountain treks
Ideal for: Hikers · Luxury seekers · Couples
Suggested stay: 3 Days
Best time: September – May
EXPLORE THE HEIGHTS →
THE EMPTY QUARTER BORDER

WAHIBA SANDS

Sharqiya Region · Desert Wilderness

Ride across copper-colored sand ridges, experience dune-bashing, sunset camel rides, and sleep in high-end desert glamping domes under a starry canopy.

Best known for: Dune safaris, Bedouin camps, Desert stars
Ideal for: Adventurers · Glampers · Families
Suggested stay: 1–2 Nights
Best time: October – March
EXPLORE THE DUNES →
HISTORIC ISLAMIC CAPITAL

NIZWA

Dakhiliyah Region · Oasis Heartland

Step back in time inside Nizwa Fort's circular clay tower, trade in the Friday cattle market, and browse silver khanjar daggers and pottery in the souks.

Best known for: Nizwa Fort, Friday Cattle Souk, Silver crafts
Ideal for: History lovers · Photographers · Collectors
Suggested stay: 2 Days
Best time: September – April
EXPLORE NIZWA →
EMERALD CANYON CANALS

WADI SHAB

Sharqiya Region · Coastal Gorges

Trek between towering canyon walls, cross freshwater rivers by small boats, and swim through crystal-clear pools to find the hidden waterfall cave.

Best known for: Canyon hikes, Freshwater caves, Sinkholes
Ideal for: Swimmers · Adventure seekers · Nature lovers
Suggested stay: 1 Day
Best time: November – March

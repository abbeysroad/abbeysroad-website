Essential Highlights & Selling Points

Key regional features, luxury accommodation standards, and seamless logistics that make Flåm & Fjords an effortless recommendation for your travelers.

Flåm Mountain Railway (Flåmsbana)

Descend 863 meters through steep ravines, roaring Kjosfossen waterfalls, and snow-capped mountain peaks.

UNESCO Nærøyfjord Electric Vessel Voyage

Cruise silently between vertical 1,400m granite cliff walls and tiny mountain farming hamlets.

Aurlandsfjord & Stegastein Viewpoint

Peer down 650 meters over fjord waters from a cantilevered wooden mountain panorama platform.

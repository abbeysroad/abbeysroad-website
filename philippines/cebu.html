THE ESSENCE OF CEBU

Queen City of the South

Cebu seamlessly combines an international airport hub, 5-star beachfront resorts in Mactan, rich Spanish colonial history, and world-class aquatic adventures along its western and southern coasts.

Moalboal Sardine Run

Swim just meters off Panagsama Beach amidst millions of swirling sardines forming shimmering underwater vortexes.

Kawasan Falls Canyoneering

Trek, cliff jump, and swim down vibrant electric-blue jungle river canyons leading into Kawasan's multi-tiered waterfalls.

Mactan Luxury Resorts

Relax in sprawling 5-star oceanfront sanctuaries featuring marine sanctuaries, private white sand beaches, and holistic spas.

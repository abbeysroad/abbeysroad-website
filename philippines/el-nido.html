THE ESSENCE OF EL NIDO

World’s Top Island Destination

Repeatedly voted among the best islands in the world by Conde Nast Traveler and Travel + Leisure, El Nido is the crown jewel of Palawan’s Bacuit Archipelago.

Secret & Big Lagoons

Squeeze through narrow rock openings into tranquil, emerald-green hidden water chambers surrounded by sheer rock walls.

Private Banca Charters

Escape the crowds with Pandora Travel's private outrigger banca charters, complete with gourmet beachfront seafood BBQs.

Limestone Archipelago

Explore over 45 pristine islands, hidden coves, sandbars, and vibrant coral gardens teeming with green sea turtles.

El Nido Concierge Guide

✈️

Getting There

Fly directly into Lio Airport (ENI) in El Nido via AirSWIFT from Manila or Cebu, or take a scenic flight into Puerto Princesa.

☀️

Best Season

November through May is dry season with calm seas, perfect for island hopping, snorkeling, and clear underwater visibility.

🍽️

Gastronomy

Enjoy freshly caught lapu-lapu fish grilled over coconut husks, beachfront wood-fired pizzas, and sunset cocktail lounges.

💡

Concierge Tip

Always book private island hopping tours (Tour A & C) to visit secret lagoons before commercial group boats arrive.

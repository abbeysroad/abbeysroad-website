THE ESSENCE OF BORACAY

World Famous Powder Sand

Famous worldwide for its 4-mile stretch of flour-soft White Beach, crystal clear shallow waters, vibrant beachfront dining, and unforgettable sunsets.

White Beach Station 1

The quietest, most luxurious stretch of White Beach, home to 5-star resorts, private beach lounges, and shallow turquoise waters.

Paraw Sunset Sailing

Board a traditional double-outrigger sailboat as the sun sets over the horizon in brilliant shades of magenta, orange, and gold.

Puka Shell Beach & Coves

Discover pristine northern coves surrounded by cliffside forests, perfect for quiet beach strolls and snorkeling away from main crowds.

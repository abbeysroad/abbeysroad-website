THE ESSENCE OF CORON

Asia’s Wreck Diving Capital

Coron Island is world-famous for its cleanest freshwater lakes in the Philippines and twelve remarkably preserved 1944 Japanese supply ships resting at shallow to recreational dive depths.

Kayangan & Barracuda Lakes

Trek up stone stairways into pristine freshwater lakes framed by sheer limestone cathedrals and underwater rock formations.

WWII Japanese Fleet Dives

Explore historic wrecks like the Akitsushima and Irako, now covered in soft corals and home to sea turtles and reef sharks.

Twin Lagoons

Swim through a narrow underwater tunnel where warm saltwater meets cool freshwater in a dramatic thermocline phenomenon.

THE ESSENCE OF BOHOL

Geological Marvels & Eco Sanctuaries

Bohol is a countryside paradise where green mountain hills turn chocolate brown in summer, rivers wind through lush jungles, and coral reefs fringe quiet white beaches.

The Chocolate Hills

Over 1,260 conical limestone mounds stretching across the horizon, forming one of UNESCO's most unique geological landscapes.

Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary

Visit Corella’s protected forest sanctuary to observe the world's smallest nocturnal primate with enormous, endearing eyes.

Panglao Island & Balicasag

Stay on Panglao's cliffside resorts and take morning boat trips to Balicasag Island Marine Sanctuary for sea turtle snorkeling.

PRACTICAL ADVICE

Bohol Concierge Guide

✈️

Getting There

Fly into Bohol-Panglao International Airport (TAG) directly from Manila or take a 2-hour OceanJet fast ferry from Cebu Pier.

☀️

Best Season

November through May provides dry, sunny weather. In summer months (March–May), the Chocolate Hills turn their signature brown hue.

🍽️

Gastronomy

Sample Kalamay (sweet coconut sticky rice dessert), fresh organic farm dishes at Bohol Bee Farm, and beachfront seafood grills.

💡

Concierge Tip

Wake up early for 6:00 AM dolphin watching off Pamilacan Island before group boats arrive at Balicasag sanctuary.

THE ESSENCE OF SIARGAO

Paradise of Palms & Waves

Voted Best Island in Asia, Siargao captures hearts with its tear-drop shape, lush palm forests, world-class surf breaks, and ultra-chic boutique island villas.

Cloud 9 Boardwalk & Surf

Watch pro surfers glide on world-famous barrel waves or take private beginner lessons on gentle outer breaks.

Sugba & Sohoton Lagoons

Paddleboard through turquoise mangrove sanctuaries, dive off timber wooden towers, and swim with stingless jellyfish.

Naked, Daku & Guyam Islands

Spend the day hopping between three distinct tropical islands featuring pure sandbars, coconut groves, and fresh grilled seafood.

LAGOON & PADDLEBOARD

Private Sugba Lagoon Stand-Up Paddleboard Safari

Cruise into Del Carmen mangrove forest aboard a private wooden banca. Enjoy private stand-up paddleboarding, diving board jumps, and a seafood picnic in the floating sanctuary house.

ISLAND EXPLORATION

Coconut Viewpoint & Maasin River Palm Swing

Drive past millions of green palms along the island highway, view the Maasin River palm swing, and swim in Magpupungko natural rock tidal pools at low tide.

Getting There

Fly directly into Sayak Airport (IAO) via direct domestic flights from Manila or Cebu. Private air-conditioned resort transfers provided.

Best Season

March to May brings calm crystal waters for lagoon paddleboarding. September to November is peak surf season for Cloud 9 waves.

🍽️

Gastronomy

Siargao is a culinary haven! Enjoy smoothie bowls, vegan sourdough bakeries, authentic Italian pasta, and fresh tuna ceviche (Kinilaw).

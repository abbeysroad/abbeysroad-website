CAPITAL & TRADITION

HANOI

Tonkin Region · Northern Vietnam

Navigate the intricate, energy-filled streets of Hanoi's Old Quarter. Experience historical landmarks, quiet lakeside walkways, and local street gastronomy from the sidecar of a vintage Minsk motorcycle.

Best known for: Hoan Kiem Lake, Old Quarter, Sidecar tours, Egg coffee
Ideal for: Cultural explorers · Food enthusiasts · Historians
Suggested stay: 2-3 Days
Best time: October – April

LIMESTONE MAJESTY

NINH BINH & HA LONG

Ninh Binh & Lan Ha Bay · Northern Vietnam

Hike Mua Cave peak, board a hand-paddled boat through Tam Coc's karst valleys, and board a premium luxury cruise across Lan Ha and Halong Bay's dramatic, emerald waters.

Best known for: Mua Cave hikes, Lan Ha Bay cruising, Tam Coc waterways
Ideal for: Nature lovers · Hikers · Luxury cruisers
Suggested stay: 3-4 Days
Best time: September – May
EXPLORE THE VALLEYS →
ANCIENT LANTERNS

HOI AN

Quang Nam Province · Central Vietnam

Walk the lantern-lit cobblestones of a historic trading port. Taste modern local gastronomy, bike through organic eco-villages, and enjoy private, romantic dinners in lush green rice fields.

Best known for: Yellow ancient walls, Lantern festivals, Rice field dining
Ideal for: Couples · Gourmet foodies · Art collectors
Suggested stay: 2-3 Days
Best time: February – August
EXPLORE HOI AN →
METROPOLIS & ARCHITECTURE

SAIGON

Ho Chi Minh City · Southern Vietnam

Contrast contemporary art gallery spaces with the historic Cu Chi Tunnels. Explore local artisanal gin distilleries and experience modern culinary masterclasses in hidden urban spaces.

Best known for: Cu Chi Tunnels, Contemporary art, Craft gin scene, Rooftop dining
Ideal for: Urban adventurers · Gin tasting · History buffs
Suggested stay: 3 Days
Best time: December – April
EXPLORE SAIGON →
SACRED ANGKOR TEMPLES

SIEM REAP, CAMBODIA

Angkor Archaeological Park · Cambodia

Stand before the towering spires of Angkor Wat. Explore jungle-clad ruins of Ta Prohm and Bayon temple, cruise Tonle Sap at sunset, and dine under starlit Cambodian rice valleys.

Best known for: Angkor Wat, Ta Prohm jungle temple, Tonle Sap cruises
Ideal for: Temple historians · Photographers · Romantics
Suggested stay: 3-4 Days
Best time: November – February
EXPLORE SIEM REAP →
METROPOLIS & SANCTUARY

BANGKOK & KOH YAO YAI

Bangkok & Phang Nga Bay · Thailand

Savor the buzzing energetic alleys of Bangkok's Chinatown and the Grand Palace. Then fly south to Phang Nga Bay, retreating to a luxury pool villa on the serene shores of Koh Yao Yai.

Best known for: Grand Palace, Chao Phraya canals, Koh Yao Yai lagoon pool villas
Ideal for: Urban explorers · Beach relaxers · Island hoppers
Suggested stay: 5-6 Days
Best time: November – March
